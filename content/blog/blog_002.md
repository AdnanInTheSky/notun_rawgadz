# Minimalist Tech Setup: Essential Gear for Developers

Your workspace directly influences your daily focus, output quality, and overall well-being. Eliminating clutter while investing in high-quality ergonomic tools is key to a sustainable workflow.

## Key Setup Pillars

1. **Ergonomic Mechanical Keyboard**: Low profile switches reduce wrist strain during long coding sessions.
2. **Precision Wireless Mouse**: High DPI sensors and customizable side buttons streamline navigation.
3. **High-Refresh Monitor**: Crisp text rendering and smooth scrolling reduce eye fatigue.

> "Simplicity is about subtracting the obvious and adding the meaningful." — John Maeda

## Final Thoughts

Focus on quality over quantity when upgrading your workstation. Each piece of gear should earn its space on your desk.
