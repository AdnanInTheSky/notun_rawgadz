## **Why Every Toyota Owner in Bangladesh Needs a Quality Steering Cover**

The steering wheel is the most handled part of any car. In a country like Bangladesh, several factors contribute to its rapid wear and tear:

1. **Humidity & Sweat:** The high humidity in BD often leads to sweaty palms. Over time, the salt and oils from your skin can erode factory leather or plastic, leading to peeling.
2. **UV Damage:** The Bangladeshi sun is unforgiving. If your car is parked outside during the day, the steering wheel can reach temperatures high enough to cause microscopic cracks in the material.
3. **Grip & Safety:** Many factory steering wheels in the Corolla or Allion series can become slippery when aged. A high-quality cover ensures that your hands never slip during an emergency maneuver in chaotic traffic.
