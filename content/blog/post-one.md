# Getting Started with Rawgad Products and Hardware

Welcome to the official guide on setting up and optimizing your Rawgad minimalist gear!

## Unboxing & First Steps

Every Rawgad product is designed to work seamlessly out of the box with zero setup hassle or software clutter.

1. **Unbox your device** and inspect the custom minimalist packaging.
2. **Connect via USB-C or Bluetooth** to your primary development workstation.
3. **Customize layout or controls** to fit your specific editing or coding workflow.
