# Minimalist Smart Watch

Matte black titanium casing with real-time biometric tracking, customizable bezel options, and 7-day battery life.

### Key Specifications
- **Display**: 1.4-inch AMOLED Always-On Retina Display (454 x 454 resolution)
- **Battery Life**: Up to 7 days continuous use on a single charge with fast magnetic induction charging
- **Biometric Sensors**: Optical heart rate monitor, SpO2 blood oxygen, sleep stage tracking, and ECG
- **Durability**: 5 ATM water resistance rated up to 50 meters, sapphire crystal glass lens
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth 5.3 Low Energy, NFC payments, and multi-satellite GPS
