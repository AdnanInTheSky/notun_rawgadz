# Retro Instant Camera

Classic analog aesthetic meets modern instant print optics for quick creative shots.

### Camera Specifications
- **Lens**: 60mm coated optical glass lens with autofocus sensor
- **Shutter Speed**: Programmed electronic shutter (1/2 sec to 1/250 sec)
- **Flash**: Built-in intelligent auto-exposure ring flash with recharge time of 0.2-6 seconds
- **Film Compatibility**: Standard i-Type and vintage 600 instant film cartridges
- **Power**: Internal rechargeable lithium-ion battery via USB-C
