# Studio ANC Headphones

Active noise cancelling wireless headphones engineered for deep focus and pristine audio.

### Acoustic Architecture
- **Drivers**: Custom 45mm dynamic neodymium drivers tuned for studio-grade acoustic linearity
- **Noise Cancellation**: Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) with 4 external reference microphones
- **Transparency Mode**: Instant ambient awareness toggle with voice enhancement algorithm
- **Battery Life**: 40 hours with ANC activated; quick 10-minute charge gives 5 hours playback
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth 5.3 with LDAC, AAC, and SBC high-resolution audio codecs
