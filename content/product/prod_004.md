# Custom Mechanical Keyboard

Hot-swappable tactile switches with customizable per-key RGB lighting, solid aluminum CNC chassis, and sound-dampening silicone foam.

### Hardware Details
- **Chassis**: Anodized 6063 aerospace aluminum with gasket-mounted brass switch plate
- **Sockets**: 5-pin hot-swappable PCB sockets compatible with standard MX footprint switches
- **Keycaps**: Double-shot PBT keycaps in Cherry/OEM profile with shine-through legends
- **Polling Rate**: 1,000Hz wired USB-C mode; 2.4GHz low-latency wireless and Bluetooth 5.1
- **Sound Profile**: Dual-layer silicone sound dampening pads with factory-lubed screw-in stabilizers
