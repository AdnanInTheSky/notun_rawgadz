# Hi-Fi Portable Speaker

Waterproof 360-degree spatial sound with 20 hours of continuous playback.

### Sound & Durability Specifications
- **Audio Output**: Dual 20W full-range acoustic drivers with twin passive bass radiators (40W Peak)
- **Water & Dust Rating**: IP67 waterproof and dustproof with drop-resistant silicone end-caps
- **Battery**: 5,200 mAh capacity delivering up to 20 hours continuous playtime; doubles as a powerbank
- **Stereo Pairing**: True Wireless Stereo (TWS) link mode to sync two speakers for stereo soundstage
- **Microphone**: Echo-cancelling speakerphone microphone for conference calls
