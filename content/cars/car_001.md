# Vehicle Overview

The **Porsche 911 GT3 RS** is engineered for maximum motorsport performance on both road and circuit:

### Engine & Performance Specifications
- **Engine**: 4.0L Naturally Aspirated Boxer-6
- **Horsepower**: 518 hp @ 8,500 rpm
- **Torque**: 342 lb-ft @ 6,300 rpm
- **0–60 mph**: 3.0 seconds
- **Top Track Speed**: 184 mph
- **Transmission**: 7-speed Porsche Doppelkupplung (PDK) with paddle shifters

### Key Aerodynamic Innovations
- Central radiator concept inspired by the Le Mans-winning 911 RSR
- Continuously adjustable active aero elements on front wing and two-part rear wing
- Drag Reduction System (DRS) operated directly from the steering wheel
- High downforce setup generating 900+ lbs of downforce at 124 mph
