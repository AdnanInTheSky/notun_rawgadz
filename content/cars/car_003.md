# Vehicle Overview

The **Audi RS6 Avant Performance** blends supercar-grade acceleration with everyday wagon practicality:

### Engine & Transmission
- **Engine**: 4.0L Twin-Turbocharged TFSI V8
- **Power**: 621 hp @ 6,000 rpm
- **Torque**: 627 lb-ft @ 2,300 rpm
- **0–60 mph**: 3.3 seconds
- **Drivetrain**: Permanent quattro All-Wheel Drive with Sport Differential
- **Transmission**: 8-speed Tiptronic Automatic with Launch Control

### Luxury & Cargo Versatility
- 30 cubic feet of cargo volume behind second row (extends to 59.3 cu ft)
- Valcona leather sports seats with honeycomb stitching and ventilation
- Bang & Olufsen 3D Advanced Sound System (1,820W, 19 speakers)
