# Vehicle Overview

The **BMW M4 Competition Coupe** delivers aggressive performance with daily precision:

### Engine & Drivetrain
- **Engine**: 3.0L BMW M TwinPower Turbo Inline-6 (S58)
- **Power**: 523 hp @ 6,250 rpm
- **Torque**: 479 lb-ft @ 2,750–5,730 rpm
- **0–60 mph**: 3.4 seconds
- **Transmission**: 8-speed M Sport Automatic with Drivelogic
- **Drivetrain**: M xDrive with selectable 4WD, 4WD Sport, and 2WD pure rear-wheel drive modes
