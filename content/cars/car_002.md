# Vehicle Overview

The **Tesla Model S Plaid** has the quickest acceleration of any vehicle in production:

### Powertrain & Performance
- **Motors**: Tri-Motor All-Wheel Drive with Carbon-Sleeved Rotors
- **Power Output**: 1,020 horsepower
- **0–60 mph**: 1.99 seconds (with rollout subtracted)
- **Quarter Mile**: 9.23 seconds @ 155 mph
- **Top Speed**: 200 mph (with Track Package hardware)
- **Range (EPA Est.)**: 359 miles

### Interior & Technology
- 17-inch cinematic display with 2200x1300 resolution
- Wireless gaming console compute power (10 teraflops)
- 22-speaker, 960-watt premium audio system with active road noise reduction
- Full Self-Driving Supervised hardware capability
